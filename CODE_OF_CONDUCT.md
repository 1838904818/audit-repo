# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in `audit-repo` to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for people of every background and experience level. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to treat one another with respect in repository spaces and in related public discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and patient when giving feedback.
- Focus disagreement on ideas, evidence, and user impact rather than people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and requests to stop an unwanted interaction.
- Remove credentials, private repository data, and personal information from examples.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and make a good-faith effort to repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the repository owner through the private contact method listed on their GitHub profile when available. Do not put personal or sensitive details in a public issue. If the concern involves an immediate safety risk, illegal content, or the repository owner, use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical, without promising an outcome before the evidence is reviewed.

Security vulnerabilities belong in [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/1838904818/audit-repo/security/advisories/new), not the conduct channel.
